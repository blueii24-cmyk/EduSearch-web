# EduSearch

> **Find what you can do next.**

EduSearch is a student-focused education and career discovery platform designed to help students understand what opportunities are available around them based on their education, skills, interests, and location.

Instead of making students search separately for colleges, jobs, internships, and career paths, EduSearch aims to connect everything into one experience.

---

## 🚀 What is EduSearch?

A student can create a profile containing:

- Current education/course
- Academic year
- Percentage/marks
- Skills
- Interests
- Location

EduSearch then helps them explore:

### 🎓 Colleges
- Nearby colleges
- Courses and degrees
- Eligibility requirements
- Fees
- Admission status
- Application information
- Eligibility estimate based on the student's profile

### 💼 Jobs
- Jobs near the student's location
- Required skills
- Salary
- Experience requirements
- Fresher-friendly opportunities
- Skill-match percentage

### 🧑‍💻 Internships
- Nearby internships
- Stipend
- Duration
- Required skills
- Fresher opportunities
- Skill-match percentage

### 🗺️ Local Discovery
EduSearch is designed around locality-based discovery.

Students can explore opportunities geographically through an interactive map experience containing:

- Colleges
- Jobs
- Internships
- Student location

The map supports:

- Zoom in
- Zoom out
- Recenter
- Panning
- Marker selection
- Map/list interaction

### 🎯 "Your Shot"

A major idea behind EduSearch is helping a student understand:

> **"Do I actually have a shot at this opportunity?"**

Instead of simply listing an opportunity, EduSearch compares the student's profile against the requirements.

Examples:

- 🟢 Good match
- 🟡 Almost ready
- ⚪ Explore

The system also shows:

- Match percentage
- Skills already possessed
- Missing skills
- Eligibility information
- Suggested next step

The goal is to help students understand not only **what exists**, but also **what they should do next**.
